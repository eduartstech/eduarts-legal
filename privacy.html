  • Certified Third-Party Payment Gateways:
    • Website & Academy Course Payments: Processed securely through certified third-party payment gateways, primarily Razorpay Software Private Limited, compliant with the highest industry security standard (PCI-DSS Level 1). 7. Artificial Intelligence & Data Safety (Google Gemini)

EduARTs utilizes state-of-the-art generative AI (Google Gemini API) to generate lesson plans, worksheets, celebration posters, and classroom activities:

  • Curriculum Descriptors Only: Prompts transmitted to the AI engine contain academic syllabus topics, grade levels, subject areas, language mediums, and event descriptions.
  • Strict Personal Data Isolation: Teacher personal information, user identities, student names, contact numbers, and lead form data are NEVER transmitted to external AI APIs.
  • No Public Model Training: We do not utilize your personal data or user-generated lesson notes to train public or foundational AI models.

10. Data Storage, Offline Caching & Technical Security

We maintain comprehensive administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between client applications, websites, and backend servers use industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.
  • Secure Credential Storage: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using modern salted hashing algorithms. Authentication tokens use secure storage APIs on mobile devices.
  • Offline-First Caching: For EduARTs Teacher OS, local lesson plans and timetable caches are stored in the private application sandbox on your device and are never accessible to other applications.
  • Access Controls: Administrative access to server infrastructure and lead records is restricted through strict multi-factor authentication and role-based permissions.

12. Account Deletion for EduARTs Teacher OS (In-App & Web)

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and data protection standards, educators registered with EduARTs Teacher OS have the absolute right to delete their accounts and personal data at any time.

Method A: In-App Account Deletion (Inside Teacher OS Mobile App)

If you have the EduARTs Teacher OS app installed on your device:

  1. Open the EduARTs app and log in to your account.
  2. Tap ക്രമീകരണങ്ങൾ (Settings) from the main navigation menu.
  3. Scroll to the മറ്റുള്ളവ (Others) section and tap അക്കൗണ്ട് സുരക്ഷ (Account Security).
  4. Select Delete Account (highlighted in red).
  5. Type DELETE in uppercase in the confirmation box.
  6. Enter your account password to verify ownership.
  7. Tap Delete Account.

Immediate System Action: The server verifies your password and confirmation text, records the deletion request, updates the account status to PENDING_DELETION, immediately revokes all active session tokens across all devices, blocks subsequent logins, and clears local user databases from your device.

Method B: External Web-Based Deletion Request (No App Required)

If you have uninstalled the app, lost your device, or prefer web-based deletion:

  1. Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Page (delete-account.html).
  2. Alternatively, email our support team directly at: eduartstech@gmail.com.
  3. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - EduARTs.
  4. Send the email from your registered EduARTs email address and include your full name and associated school name.
  5. Our team will verify account ownership and process the deletion request.
